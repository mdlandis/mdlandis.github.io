Personal Project

Tactics Beyond Time

A Teamfight Tactics inspired single-player autobattler. Features 65 playable characters, 55 items, and 23 traits, all fully functional. Check out the demo!

I embarked on this project to familiarize myself with Unreal Engine and to challenge myself to finish a substantial, highly functional project.

The game is a none-too-subtle clone of Teamfight Tactics - I didn’t want to get bogged down too much in trying to design something novel, and instead based the game around a system that I knew for a fact would work at scale. Even within the confines of making a TFT clone, I had plenty of opportunities to exercise design skills, in the individual designs of traits and units as well as decisions I made with regards to adapting the game to single player.

The characters in this game (and the names of the items) are player characters from D&D games I’ve participated in over the years. I had a fun time interviewing players about their characters and what they were best known for, and putting the lore blurbs they gave me into the game. Character models were built on and purchased from Heroforge, and much of the portrait art was hand-drawn by fellow players (thank you especially to Tyler and Evelyn!!)

I did not sell or distribute this project publicly. This game is a love letter to TFT and D&D, a challenge for myself, and a gift for my friends.
